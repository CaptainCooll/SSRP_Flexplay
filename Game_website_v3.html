FlexPlay

Hand-rehabilitation games for arthritis, driven by the FlexPlay sensor glove. Each game is one prescribed exercise, so playing the game is doing the exercise.

Participant

an anonymous code such as P01, never a name

Research prototype, not a medical device. Taking part is voluntary and you can press Stop at any moment. Never push into pain.

Input

Simulator active

The glove streams one checked line per sample at 100 Hz: seq, t_ms, flex×5 (thumb→little), fsr×5 (index, middle, ring, little tips, ball), flags*checksum, analog values in millivolts. The baud rate (default 460800) is set in Therapist settings. Use Chrome or Edge for USB serial. Lost or corrupt lines are counted, and the game pauses by itself if the glove goes silent.

Signal Lab

raw vs filtered · noise · recording

See what the sensors really output, find the noise, and record data for the report (the course brief asks for at least 5 minutes per condition). Works with the glove or the keyboard simulator.

Amplitude spectrum of the last 5 s (dB re 1 mV). At 100 Hz sampling the axis ends at 50 Hz: mains hum at 50 Hz lands exactly on the right edge, and its 100 Hz and 200 Hz harmonics fold down to 0 Hz as slow drift. Hand movement sits below about 5 Hz.

IMU Lab

MPU-6050 accelerometer (±4 g) and gyroscope (±500 °/s) at 100 Hz: live scope, spectrum, tremor-band metrics, orientation and condition-labelled recording. With no glove connected it runs on a simulated hand.

Spectrum of the last 5 s of the raw signal (dB re 1 unit). Gravity (0 Hz) is removed. Parkinsonian rest tremor is typically 4–6 Hz and essential or physiological tremor 6–12 Hz; the 4–12 Hz band is reported. The MPU-6050 low-pass is set to 44 Hz in the firmware, so mains and vibration above that is attenuated before sampling.

Therapist settings

Difficulty

Adaptive: after each game the level moves up, stays or moves down from the score and from how the second half compared with the first (rule from Lahiri et al., 2013). The targets above are the Medium level.

Signal processing

Applied to the glove signals before any game sees them. Change it to compare raw against filtered; the Signal Lab shows the effect live.

Sensor constants

FSR force ≈ k × conductance. Replace k with the values from your known-weight calibration (analyze_flexplay.py fsrcal). Set Vcc to the 3V3 rail you measured with a multimeter.
